# D7. Solver formulation (M2)

Decision D7 of the OpenSolve Pipe decision record ([index](README.md)). Decisions override the specs; `docs/GOALS.md` overrides decisions. Code and tests cite this decision as `CLAUDE.md D7`.

---

- The nodal unknown is the **energy grade line** (EGL = z + p/ρg + v²/2g), so pump head and fitting losses enter exactly; HGL and static pressure are derived with the velocity in the connected pipe. Reservoirs and reference nodes are quiescent boundaries (v = 0). EPANET neglects velocity heads, so EPANET comparisons compare our EGL with its heads. *(Correctness; GOALS §4.1.)*
- Transition zone 2000 ≤ Re < 4000: EPANET 2.2's Dunlop cubic (Hermite in Re/2000 between laminar and the turbulent formula in use), flagged `TRANSITIONAL_FLOW`. `swamee_jain` mode uses EPANET's exact 5.74/Re^0.9 form so EPANET tests compare network algorithms, not friction formulas.
- Branch legs are imposed exactly as head offsets on a merged head unknown, not iterated with a Jacobian floor (a floor stalled Newton on large mains feeding small tubing).
- Unknown pipe materials, schedules or sizes are topology issues, reported together with the other topology problems.
- **Stop test and flag (METHOD_VERSION 1.2.0, change set C0b):** a link's energy residual passes at 10⁻⁷ m + 4ε·max(\|H_start\|, \|H_end\|, \|h\|), ε = 2⁻⁵², since 10⁻⁷ m alone is below one unit in the last place of heads from 2²⁹ m and inner solves at large heads stalled at that round-off floor; the `NOT_CONVERGED` flag names a residual's location only where that residual exceeds its stop tolerance, and where the status updates keep returning to a failed state it reports the residuals of the solve its message describes (proposal `vfd-slice2-discharge-pressure.md` §2.11.10; TSD §5.2, §10.4). *(Correctness and traceability; GOALS §3, §4.2.)*
- **A value that is not a finite number names its law (TSD §12 item 19, 2026-11-03):** where an inner solve stops because a value of its iterate is not a finite number ("the iteration diverged (non-finite head loss)"), the message goes on to name, ids quoted, every element whose own law gave one, in link order and each element once: what was not finite (the head loss, the slope dh/dQ or both; a branch leg's head offset; an active PRV's or PSV's held head), the flow it was taken at, and, where the law has one, its loss coefficient c of h = cQ\|Q\| (a valve's K/(2gA²), an equipment item's c, an orifice plate's K/(2gA²) at that flow, a connection's fittings' Σ n K/(2gA²) where that term is the one not finite). A law whose computation raises `ArithmeticError` or `ValueError` at an iterate (fluids' Clamond near Re 10³⁰⁸, its ISO 5167-2 C where Re_D overflows at that flow, with a fluid at the schema's bounds) counts as not finite there and is named with the exception's name, never its text (an internal error, 500, before). The flag's `details` add `nonFiniteLawAt` (every id) and the first one's `nonFiniteLawFlow` and `nonFiniteLawCoefficient`, finite values only; its `componentId` and `connectionId` stay null (the flag is the network's). This is the residual-location rule's companion: the largest residual can lie on another element than the one whose law failed (verified: V1's slope overflowed while the residual named V2). Where no law gave the value, an energy balance did, and since 1.6.2 the message names it (below); to 1.6.1 it named nothing. Every quadratic law's slope is (2c)\|Q\|, finite exactly when c ≤ half the largest double (D9), so a c past that bound is refused before the solve rather than named. An orifice plate's law forms 2K, so its bound is on K, taken at the upper bound of its discharge coefficient over every flow (D20 rule (d)); that bound need not be reached, so a plate whose K passes the bound only at the C its law takes, at some flows or at every flow, is named, not refused. Here a solve that did not converge changes only its text, unless an element in it has a c past the slope bound, which is now refused before the solve (a 422; D9, D20: a main-line valve or equipment item past it diverged at its first iterate on `7fcbdb8`). A law that raised, an internal error (500) before, now gives a solve that did not converge, naming the law, unless the input makes the law unrepresentable at every flow, which is now refused: a pipe whose A ν underflows (`PIPE_SIZE_OUT_OF_RANGE`), or a plate whose π D μ is 0 or, where ISO 5167-2's eq. (4) is used, whose (π D²/4) ρ is 0 or 4ρ/(π D μ) is not finite (D20 rule (e)). The methods are 1.6.1 for D9's and D20's refusals of an element the solve never evaluates (corpus comparison against `7fcbdb8`, system curves: identical 5,189, different 0, with no ignores before the bump and only `methodVersion` and `trace.methods.methodVersion` ignored after it; against main `21230f0` on the merge, identical 5,189 with only those two fields ignored; TSD §10.4, §12 item 19). *(Traceability; GOALS §4.2.)*
- **A boundary head that is not a finite number is refused (METHOD_VERSION 1.6.2; proposal `open-defects-2.md` §3 A2a; TSD §5.1, §12 item 19).** The changelog: "A reservoir or reference node whose fixed head, its surface or port elevation plus its gauge pressure over ρg, is not a finite number (a density near the least the schema allows) is refused as a topology issue (BOUNDARY_HEAD_OUT_OF_RANGE), wherever it is: every solve of such a project diverged at its first iteration, naming nothing." As built:
  - `builder.boundary_head` gives both fixed heads by the expressions the build used to 1.6.1, term for term: the liquid surface (elevation + level) + p_s/(ρg) for a reservoir, the port elevation + p/(ρg) for a reference node, with ρg as the build forms it. `test_boundary_head.py` reads the built network's fixed heads against those closed forms bit for bit; it passes on `593378d` too.
  - `_unrepresentable_boundaries` refuses a head that is not finite as `BOUNDARY_HEAD_OUT_OF_RANGE` (422, listed after `RELIEF_VALVE_OUT_OF_RANGE`, one issue per component), wherever the component is, closed off or not. The text quotes the pressure and the density: "reservoir 'R1': a surface pressure of 1e+08 Pa(g) with the fluid's density of 5.67236e-302 kg/m³ gives a pressure head p/(ρ g) above the largest floating-point number, 1.79769e+308 m, in magnitude, so its head is not a finite floating-point number; the solver cannot represent this boundary". Every build meets it: the solve, system curves, scenarios, pump-group curves and goal seek's trials (D16 refuses a bound that reaches it).
  - The edges are adjacent doubles, bisected in `test_boundary_head.py` with fluids' g and the largest double: at 1e8 Pa(g) the head is not finite at ρ 5.672359721482875·10⁻³⁰² kg/m³ and finite at 5.672359721482876·10⁻³⁰²; at −1e5 Pa(g), at 5.672359721482875·10⁻³⁰⁵ and 5.672359721482876·10⁻³⁰⁵. At the finite edge a reservoir at 1e8 Pa(g) behind 10 m of pipe and a plug solves to zero flow with every EGL the largest double (reference case `boundary_head_at_largest_double`).
  - Not refused: an active PRV's or PSV's setpoint head that overflows (the status rules leave the active state and the case converges; four regression cases), a relief valve's set head (already `RELIEF_VALVE_OUT_OF_RANGE`, D23) and an active check valve's cracking head (named as its law; proposal §7).
- **An energy balance that is not a finite number is named (1.6.2, A2a).** The changelog: "An iteration that diverges where every law gives a finite value now names each element whose energy balance is not a finite number, with the EGLs at its ends and the component that fixes the head there (nonFiniteBalanceAt)." As built:
  - The balances are formed only where `nonfinite_laws` finds no law, head offset or held head that is not finite, so a law is still named first. A balance h − (H_start − H_end) then overflows: a head near the largest double against a large loss, or two fixed heads of opposite sign whose difference overflows.
  - The message starts `gga.DIVERGED_BALANCE`, "the iteration diverged (non-finite energy balance)". `balance_message` names each such link (`gga.NonFiniteBalance`), ids quoted, in link order, each element once, with the EGLs at its start and its end, its head loss and its flow, and the component that fixes the head the overflow comes from: a reservoir, a reference node or an active PRV or PSV, at each end without whose head the balance would be finite, at both where neither alone is enough (`_holder_needed`). For example "…: not a finite number in the energy balance h − (H_start − H_end) of 'L1' (an EGL of -1.79769e+308 m at its start, fixed by 'R1', and of 0 m at its end, with a head loss of 1.7534e+304 m at a flow of 0.00239 m³/s)".
  - The flag's details list them as `nonFiniteBalanceAt`, never as `nonFiniteLawAt`, `nonFiniteLawFlow` or `nonFiniteLawCoefficient`: their laws gave finite numbers. With this, `DIVERGED` no longer stands alone.
- **A singular Newton step is named, not solved (1.6.2; proposal `open-defects-2.md` §3 A2b).** The changelog: "Where a Newton step's matrix is singular in floating point, the message and the not-converged flag name each link whose weight 1/(dh/dQ) is lost in rounding where it alone joins free nodes to every fixed head (for example a valve with a loss coefficient above about 3·10¹⁹ s²/m⁵ before an assigned flow or a dead end), with its slope, flow and loss coefficient, and say which iteration's flows they are. The step is unchanged, so such a solve still does not converge." As built:
  - Where SuperLU finds the step's Schur matrix A21 D⁻¹ A12 singular, `gga.lost_weights` finds each link whose weight w = 1/(dh/dQ) is lost at one of its ends: fl(S + w) = S, S the other links' weights there summed once (`math.fsum`). That is exact for that sum, which is the matrix's own entry where two links meet (a bridge's far end), across a binade too.
  - A link is named only where it alone joins a part of the free nodes to the fixed heads. A column is grounded by a link from a fixed head, or from a grounded column, whose weight is kept at that column; a link is named where its weight is lost at an ungrounded end whose other end is grounded or a fixed head. The coupling of an active PRV or PSV, which makes the matrix non-symmetric, is not examined.
  - `singular_message` quotes each one (`gga.LostWeight`) with the slope the Jacobian used, its flow and its law's loss coefficient, and says which iterate: "in Newton iteration 1, at the starting flows, the weight 1/(dh/dQ) of 'V1' (a slope of 8.46e+17 s/m² at a flow of 0.00239 m³/s; loss coefficient K/(2 g A²) = 1.76758e+20 s²/m⁵) is lost in rounding: …". Past the first step it says "at the flows after iteration n", and "the previous solve's flows" where the inner solve started from flows handed in (after a status change in the outer loop, or a warm start).
  - The flag's details add `singularStepAt` (the ids, comma-separated) and the first one's `singularStepFlow`, `singularStepSlope` and `singularStepCoefficient`, finite values only. Where no lost weight is found, the message stays "the linear system could not be solved (singular matrix)".
  - The first step's threshold is a closed form (`test_singular_step.py`, its weights from fluids' Clamond): on 10 m of 0.1 m pipe at the 1 ft/s start flow, Q₀ = 0.002393893602035422 m³/s, the pipe's weight 0.114133 lies in [2⁻⁴, 2⁻³), so a valve's weight 1/(2cQ₀) is lost past c* = 2⁵⁶/Q₀ = 3.0100583407992963·10¹⁹ s²/m⁵. The step is singular at c*(1 + 10⁻⁹) and taken at c*(1 − 10⁻⁹), before an assigned flow and before a plug alike.
  - The step itself is unchanged, so such a case still ends not converged though its steady state is determined; a step for the failure branch is the proposal's §7. Measured on the build over the 56 fuzz seeds whose solves meet a singular inner step: 57 such steps, 26 named and 31 with the plain message, none of them a solve's final result.
