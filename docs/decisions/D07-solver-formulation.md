# D7. Solver formulation (M2)

Decision D7 of the OpenSolve Pipe decision record ([index](README.md)). Decisions override the specs; `docs/GOALS.md` overrides decisions. Code and tests cite this decision as `CLAUDE.md D7`.

---

- The nodal unknown is the **energy grade line** (EGL = z + p/ρg + v²/2g), so pump head and fitting losses enter exactly; HGL and static pressure are derived with the velocity in the connected pipe. Reservoirs and reference nodes are quiescent boundaries (v = 0). EPANET neglects velocity heads, so EPANET comparisons compare our EGL with its heads. *(Correctness; GOALS §4.1.)*
- Transition zone 2000 ≤ Re < 4000: EPANET 2.2's Dunlop cubic (Hermite in Re/2000 between laminar and the turbulent formula in use), flagged `TRANSITIONAL_FLOW`. `swamee_jain` mode uses EPANET's exact 5.74/Re^0.9 form so EPANET tests compare network algorithms, not friction formulas.
- Branch legs are imposed exactly as head offsets on a merged head unknown, not iterated with a Jacobian floor (a floor stalled Newton on large mains feeding small tubing).
- Unknown pipe materials, schedules or sizes are topology issues, reported together with the other topology problems.
- **Stop test and flag (METHOD_VERSION 1.2.0, change set C0b):** a link's energy residual passes at 10⁻⁷ m + 4ε·max(\|H_start\|, \|H_end\|, \|h\|), ε = 2⁻⁵², since 10⁻⁷ m alone is below one unit in the last place of heads from 2²⁹ m and inner solves at large heads stalled at that round-off floor; the `NOT_CONVERGED` flag names a residual's location only where that residual exceeds its stop tolerance, and where the status updates keep returning to a failed state it reports the residuals of the solve its message describes (proposal `vfd-slice2-discharge-pressure.md` §2.11.10; TSD §5.2, §10.4). *(Correctness and traceability; GOALS §3, §4.2.)*
- **A value that is not a finite number names its law (open item 2, 2026-11-03):** where an inner solve stops because a value of its iterate is not a finite number ("the iteration diverged (non-finite head loss)"), the message goes on to name, ids quoted, every element whose own law gave one, in link order and each element once: what was not finite (the head loss, the slope dh/dQ or both; a branch leg's head offset; an active PRV's or PSV's held head), the flow it was taken at, and, where the law has one, its loss coefficient c of h = cQ\|Q\| (a valve's K/(2gA²), an equipment item's c, an orifice plate's K/(2gA²) at that flow, a connection's fittings' Σ n K/(2gA²) where that term is the one not finite). A law whose computation raises `ArithmeticError` or `ValueError` at an iterate (fluids' Clamond near Re 10³⁰⁸, its ISO 5167-2 C at an infinite Re, with a fluid at the schema's bounds) counts as not finite there and is named with the exception's name, never its text (an internal error, 500, before). The flag's `details` add `nonFiniteLawAt` (every id) and the first one's `nonFiniteLawFlow` and `nonFiniteLawCoefficient`, finite values only; its `componentId` and `connectionId` stay null (the flag is the network's). This is the residual-location rule's companion: the largest residual can lie on another element than the one whose law failed (verified: V1's slope overflowed while the residual named V2). Where no law gave the value, only a head (a reservoir's p/(ρg) with ρ near the smallest double), the message names nothing. Every quadratic law's slope is (2c)\|Q\|, finite exactly when c ≤ half the largest double (D9), so a c past that bound is refused before the solve rather than named. Here only the text of a solve that did not converge changes; the methods are 1.6.1 for D9's refusals (corpus comparison against `7fcbdb8`, system curves, no ignores: identical 5,189, different 0; TSD §12 item 18). *(Traceability; GOALS §4.2.)*
