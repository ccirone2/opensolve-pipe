# D7. Solver formulation (M2)

Decision D7 of the OpenSolve Pipe decision record ([index](README.md)). Decisions override the specs; `docs/GOALS.md` overrides decisions. Code and tests cite this decision as `CLAUDE.md D7`.

---

- The nodal unknown is the **energy grade line** (EGL = z + p/ρg + v²/2g), so pump head and fitting losses enter exactly; HGL and static pressure are derived with the velocity in the connected pipe. Reservoirs and reference nodes are quiescent boundaries (v = 0). EPANET neglects velocity heads, so EPANET comparisons compare our EGL with its heads. *(Correctness; GOALS §4.1.)*
- Transition zone 2000 ≤ Re < 4000: EPANET 2.2's Dunlop cubic (Hermite in Re/2000 between laminar and the turbulent formula in use), flagged `TRANSITIONAL_FLOW`. `swamee_jain` mode uses EPANET's exact 5.74/Re^0.9 form so EPANET tests compare network algorithms, not friction formulas.
- Branch legs are imposed exactly as head offsets on a merged head unknown, not iterated with a Jacobian floor (a floor stalled Newton on large mains feeding small tubing).
- Unknown pipe materials, schedules or sizes are topology issues, reported together with the other topology problems.
- **Stop test and flag (METHOD_VERSION 1.2.0, change set C0b):** a link's energy residual passes at 10⁻⁷ m + 4ε·max(\|H_start\|, \|H_end\|, \|h\|), ε = 2⁻⁵², since 10⁻⁷ m alone is below one unit in the last place of heads from 2²⁹ m and inner solves at large heads stalled at that round-off floor; the `NOT_CONVERGED` flag names a residual's location only where that residual exceeds its stop tolerance, and where the status updates keep returning to a failed state it reports the residuals of the solve its message describes (proposal `vfd-slice2-discharge-pressure.md` §2.11.10; TSD §5.2, §10.4). *(Correctness and traceability; GOALS §3, §4.2.)*
- **A value that is not a finite number names its law (open item 2, 2026-11-03):** where an inner solve stops because a value of its iterate is not a finite number ("the iteration diverged (non-finite head loss)"), the message goes on to name, ids quoted, every element whose own law gave one, in link order and each element once: what was not finite (the head loss, the slope dh/dQ or both; a branch leg's head offset; an active PRV's or PSV's held head), the flow it was taken at, and, where the law has one, its loss coefficient c of h = cQ\|Q\| (a valve's K/(2gA²), an equipment item's c, an orifice plate's K/(2gA²) at that flow, a connection's fittings' Σ n K/(2gA²) where that term is the one not finite). A law whose computation raises `ArithmeticError` or `ValueError` at an iterate (fluids' Clamond near Re 10³⁰⁸, its ISO 5167-2 C where Re_D overflows at that flow, with a fluid at the schema's bounds) counts as not finite there and is named with the exception's name, never its text (an internal error, 500, before). The flag's `details` add `nonFiniteLawAt` (every id) and the first one's `nonFiniteLawFlow` and `nonFiniteLawCoefficient`, finite values only; its `componentId` and `connectionId` stay null (the flag is the network's). This is the residual-location rule's companion: the largest residual can lie on another element than the one whose law failed (verified: V1's slope overflowed while the residual named V2). Where no law gave the value, only a head (a reservoir's p/(ρg) with ρ near the smallest double), the message names nothing. Every quadratic law's slope is (2c)\|Q\|, finite exactly when c ≤ half the largest double (D9), so a c past that bound is refused before the solve rather than named. An orifice plate's law forms 2K, so its bound is on K, taken at the upper bound of its discharge coefficient over every flow (D20 rule (d)); that bound need not be reached, so a plate whose K passes the bound only at the C its law takes, at some flows or at every flow, is named, not refused. Here a solve that did not converge changes only its text, unless an element in it has a c past the slope bound, which is now refused before the solve (a 422; D9, D20: a main-line valve or equipment item past it diverged at its first iterate on `7fcbdb8`). A law that raised, an internal error (500) before, now gives a solve that did not converge, naming the law, unless the input makes the law unrepresentable at every flow, which is now refused: a pipe whose A ν underflows (`PIPE_SIZE_OUT_OF_RANGE`), or a plate whose π D μ is 0 or, where ISO 5167-2's eq. (4) is used, whose (π D²/4) ρ is 0 or 4ρ/(π D μ) is not finite (D20 rule (e)). The methods are 1.6.1 for D9's and D20's refusals of an element the solve never evaluates (corpus comparison against `7fcbdb8`, system curves: identical 5,189, different 0, with no ignores before the bump and only `methodVersion` and `trace.methods.methodVersion` ignored after it; TSD §10.4, §12 item 18). *(Traceability; GOALS §4.2.)*
