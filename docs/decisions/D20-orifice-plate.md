# D20. Orifice plate (owner, 2026-10-27)

Decision D20 of the OpenSolve Pipe decision record ([index](README.md)). Decisions override the specs; `docs/GOALS.md` overrides decisions. Code and tests cite this decision as `CLAUDE.md D20`.

Design proposal: [`orifice-plate.md`](../proposals/orifice-plate.md).

---

Built from `docs/proposals/orifice-plate.md` (physics, sources and web designers, a synthesizer, two reviewers, a reviser and the owner's answers of 2026-10-27 in its §10; then a contract builder, API, reference, corpus-tool and web builders, and integrators; its §12 records the as-built departures). Specs: PRD §3.1.2, SDD §4.16, TSD §4.10.

### Model

`Orifice`, type `orifice`, ports `inlet` → `outlet`: a square-edged concentric plate, a restriction orifice (a minimum-flow bypass, balancing) or an orifice flow meter, one element for both (owner). A required `boreDiameter` d (m, (0, 10], no default); an optional `dischargeCoefficient`: absent, ISO 5167-2's Reader-Harris/Gallagher C at the solved flow, the cited default (labelled "default", D10); a number in (0, 1], an entered C from a calibration or datasheet, user data: ISO 5167-2 eq. (1)'s C at the plate's taps (at D and D/2 tappings for a plate without taps), held at every flow, not the flow coefficient α = C/√(1 − β⁴) (the schema, form and trace say so); optional `taps` (`corner`, `flange` or `d_and_d_2`): absent, a plate without taps, with no reading. No status (valves isolate it) and no Cv or Kv (owner): a restriction known only by a Cv, a Kv or a drop at a flow is D18's equipment, and both forms' hints say so. D is the inner diameter of the inlet pipe's end segment at the inlet port, as a valve's reference diameter (D9); β = d/D. Each value's only rule bounds it, so no new `x-` extension; `schemaVersion` stays 1 (an additive type, D14). *(GOALS §4.1: no source gives a default bore; ISO 5167-2 is the cited source of C.)*

### Law

h = EGL_in − EGL_out = K(C_L)·v\|v\|/(2g), v = Q/A on D (`services/solver/orifice.py`, `LinkKind.ORIFICE`): K = (S/(Cβ²) − 1)², S = √(1 − β⁴(1 − C²)), ISO 5167-2's pressure-loss relation over ρv²/2 (fluids `discharge_coefficient_to_K`), computed without its cancellation as β → 1 (K = u², u = (1 − β⁴)/(Cβ²(S + Cβ²)), 1 − β = (D − d)/D). **One loss per plate** (owner): C_L, the C that sets the loss, is the C at D and D/2 tappings whatever the plate's taps: ISO 5167-2:2003 eq. (4) (fluids `C_Reader_Harris_Gallagher`) at Re_eff = max(Re_D, Re_lo), or an entered C_e moved there, C_L = C_e·r with r = C_ISO(D and D/2)/C_ISO(taps) at the same Re_eff for corner or flange taps (C_e itself without taps or at D and D/2 taps), so a calibration equal to ISO's C gives ISO's answer. Below ISO's lowest Reynolds number, Re_lo = max(5000, 16000β²) (ISO's limit made continuous at β 0.56) or max(5000, 170000β²D) at flange taps, C is held at its value there and flagged (WANDA's practice); fluids is called with the mass flow whose Re_D is Re_eff, never at Q = 0 or below Re 5000. The slope is exact (eq. (4)'s analytic dC/dRe, dK/dC = −2K(1/C + β²/S)), zero flow takes the valve path (the Jacobian floor; the law stays exact), and `gga.py`, `status.py` and `system_curve.py` are unchanged. Named departures, stated in the methods summary and the trace at their largest over ISO 5167-2's range (D 50 mm–1 m, β 0.1–0.75, Re_D from Re_lo up): ISO's relation read with a meter's own C (a K up to 6.14 % higher at corner and 6.34 % at flange tappings, a flow up to 2.94 % and 3.03 % lower where the plate takes the whole head; up to β 0.6, 0.64 % and 0.84 % in K); Reader-Harris et al. 2019's C at L2′ = 0.125 (a K 0.063–1.24 % lower, a flow up to 0.63 % higher); the continuous Re_lo (stricter than ISO's by up to 17.6, only for 0.559 < β ≤ 0.56). *(GOALS §4.1: exact, and checkable by hand.)*

### Reading and vena contracta

A meter's `differentialPressure` = (1 − β⁴)/(C_M²β⁴)·ρv\|v\|/2, ISO 5167-2 eq. (1) with ε = 1 (liquids only), C_M ISO's C at the plate's own taps (held at their own Re_lo) or the entered C as entered; null without taps; it does not act on the network. p_vc = p_up + ρg(z_up − z_down) − (1 − β⁴)/(C_L²β⁴)·ρv²/2, p_up the static pressure of the port the flow enters and z_down the elevation of the port it leaves by (WANDA); below the vapor pressure (≤ 0 Pa absolute for a fluid without one) it raises `BELOW_VAPOR_PRESSURE` (invalid) on the component: a flashing jet chokes the flow, so the flow solved is too high. None at zero flow or in a floating section; incipient cavitation is not checked. *(GOALS §4.6.)*

### Limits, flagged and refused

`CORRELATION_OUT_OF_RANGE` (warning, one per inequality, `details.limit` naming it in SI): wherever eq. (4) is used (ISO's C, or a moved entered C), d < 12.5 mm, D outside 50–1000 mm, β outside 0.1–0.75 (ISO 5167-2's limits from fluids' documentation), and 0 < Re_D below Re_lo; for every plate, an outlet pipe of another bore than D (the relation assumes recovery in the inlet's bore) and reverse flow (the symmetric law on D); with an entered C, laminar flow, 0 < Re_D < 2000 in the inlet pipe, as a valve's Cv. The plate and its installation are assumed to meet ISO 5167-2 and are not checked. `ORIFICE_OUT_OF_RANGE` (422, listed after `EQUIPMENT_RATING_OUT_OF_RANGE`, one per plate, quoting d, D, β and the bound broken), for a plate the law cannot represent at any flow (rule (d)'s first test, at the least C the law takes at any flow, can refuse a plate whose flows never reach that C; below): β ≥ 1; eq. (4), where used, able to exceed 1 (from about β 0.970 on ISO's C, lower with flange taps on a pipe below about 55 mm), the text saying what it is used for there (`Refusal.basis`): at D and D/2 tappings it sets the loss, and C above 1 would be a jet larger than the bore, a loss below Borda–Carnot's; at a meter's taps (the reading) or in an entered C's move it is eq. (4) extrapolated far beyond ISO's range; each with a remedy that works for the plate; an entered C moved from corner or flange taps able to exceed 1; a loss coefficient K/(2gA²) that is not a finite positive double at the least C_L the law takes at any flow (since 1.6.2; below), or a finite one whose K at the largest C_L, an upper bound of every C the law takes up to the rounding of eq. (4) (ISO's largest bound, C_e·r_max, or C_e used as entered), is above half the largest double (`SLOPE_COEFFICIENT_MAX`, `Refusal.rule` `slope`): K is then at least that at every flow (where the rounding puts a C a few units in the last place above that bound, K is within rounding of it and the slope's dK/dC term overflows instead), and `OrificeLaw` forms 2K before 1/(2gA²), so its slope is not a finite number at any flow but zero (D9's derivation; TSD §12 item 19, 2026-11-03; the text "with a discharge coefficient of at most {C} at every flow it gives a loss coefficient K of at least {K}, …"). A K past the bound only at a smaller C is not refused, since a moved C's lower bound C_e·r_min can lie below every C its law takes (taken there, the rule refused plates whose 2K is finite at every flow, some of which solved on `7fcbdb8`; fixed the same day): a solve that reaches such a C names the plate (D7). The upper bound need not be reached (r_max is 1 for a moved C at corner or flange taps on D 1 m with a 0.5 m bore), so a plate the rule accepts can still have K past the bound at every flow, and a solve that evaluates it stops at its first iterate, naming it. Rule (d)'s first test is taken at c_low first, a closed-form lower bound of C (ISO's smallest bound, or C_e·r_min), as it was to 1.6.1, so it passes every plate it passed then. Since 1.6.2 (proposal `open-defects-2.md` §3 A1), where it fails there it is retaken at the least C_L the law takes at any flow (`orifice.least_coefficient`), up to the rounding of eq. (4), and the refusal quotes that C ("with a discharge coefficient down to {C}"; c_low where that least cannot be computed). The least C_L is:
- for an entered C used as entered, C_e;
- for a C entered at corner or flange taps, C_e times the least ratio r of the two tap sets' eq. (4) over Re ∈ [Re_lo, ∞) (`least_ratio`): r is least at either end or where dr/dx = 0, x = Re^(−1/10); eq. (4) is a sparse polynomial in x (`rhg_powers`), and the roots of dr/dx's numerator are found by a Rolle chain with bisection (`positive_roots`). The least can lie inside the range: flange taps, D 21.7 mm, β 0.5525, 2.988·10⁻⁷ below r(∞) and 3.498·10⁻⁷ below r(Re_lo) (`test_orifice_refusal_bounds.py`, C17);
- for ISO's C, eq. (4) at D and D/2 tappings at infinite Re, least over [Re_lo, ∞) by the argument in `least_coefficient`'s docstring, its constants rounded to the safe side, for β up to 0.9785, above rule (b)'s limit of about 0.9706.

New refusals are a subset of the old by construction, and goal seek's bound builds stay monotone in C_e, since the least r does not depend on C_e. The plate the test refused before (D 0.2 m, bore 0.1 m, flange taps, C_e 2.0773717524832402·10⁻¹⁵³) is accepted and converges in 507 iterations in 1000 kg/m³ and 10 Pa·s (in water it is not converged, naming the plate); one double below that C_e it is refused, quoting C_e times its least r (0.9994208818853716, at Re_lo), 2.07617·10⁻¹⁵³. The test still refuses a plate whose flows never reach that C, as decision 17 means it to (finite at every flow, whatever the fluid): a C entered at corner taps whose least ratio is at infinite Re, in a liquid viscous enough to hold C_L at every flow (D 0.1 m, bore 0.01 m, C_e 2.145282309663566·10⁻¹⁵¹: refused, though with the test bypassed its line converges in 505 iterations in 10 Pa·s). At 10 of 449 bisected edges (β ≥ 0.9), eq. (4)'s rounding puts the law's own least C 0.6 to 1.5 units in the last place below C_e times the least r, where its K/(2gA²) is not finite, and the plate is accepted. A solve that reaches that C either converges at flow 0, where `OrificeLaw` forms K·Q|Q| before it divides by 2gA² (so the loss is 0), or stops naming the plate (D7); no converged solve at a positive flow was found there (review of 2026-11-26: in 473 of 1,200 liquids tried on the edge plate of D 0.1 m, bore 0.093 m, corner taps, the factor at the C its law holds was not finite, and its line converged at flow 0 in 800 kg/m³ and 0.0518 Pa·s; open-defects-2.md §7). Nine of them, solved at their edge in 10 Pa·s, converged at flow 0; in water 7 were not converged, naming the plate (measured).

Each extreme over Re ∈ [Re_lo, ∞) is a closed-form bound (each Re-dependent term of eq. (4) at its safe end), and the least C_L is eq. (4)'s transcription alone (`rhg_terms`), so these rules do not depend on the fluid. One more, rule (e) (`Refusal.rule` `fluid`, `orifice.fluid_refusal`; 2026-11-03), takes the case's fluid: a plate whose Reynolds-number arithmetic a density or viscosity at the schema's bounds makes unrepresentable: π D μ that is 0 (every plate's law divides by it); where eq. (4) is used, (π D²/4) ρ that is 0 (fluids divides by it) or 4ρ/(π D μ) that is not finite (Re_D infinite at every flow but zero, where fluids cannot evaluate eq. (4)); a law that cannot be built, ISO's C at Re_lo raising in fluids, reached where fluids' velocity there, μ Re_lo/(D ρ), overflows while 4ρ/(π D μ) stays finite (1e-302 kg/m³ and 1e3 Pa·s on a 1 m pipe, bore 0.5 m; `test_a_plate_whose_iso_c_raises_at_its_lowest_reynolds_number_is_refused`); and, on ISO's C at corner or flange taps, a meter's coefficient C_M that fluids cannot evaluate at those taps' own Re_lo, where the results hold it (flange tappings' 170000 β² D can be far above the loss's: 42,500 against 5,000 at bore 0.5 m on a 1 m pipe in 1e-301 kg/m³ and 1e3 Pa·s; review of 1.6.1, 2026-11-17, `test_a_plate_whose_meter_c_raises_at_its_flange_taps_lowest_reynolds_number_is_refused`). The first two and the fourth were an internal error (500) before wherever the plate was, its law raising when built; the third was one wherever the solve evaluated the plate at a flow, and the fifth wherever the results gave its reading (a converged solve); a plate the solve or the results never evaluate (for example in a section that closed valves close off: isolated, locked closed or failed closed) solved before, as a system curve through the fifth did, and is now refused, as rule (e) holds wherever the plate is: with D9's refusals, the reason the methods are 1.6.1 (`test_a_closed_off_plate_with_an_infinite_reynolds_number_is_refused`, `test_a_closed_off_plate_whose_meter_c_raises_is_refused`). The first three depend on neither the bore nor C; the last two depend on the bore through Re_lo(β), which does not decrease as the bore grows, where fluids' velocity grows with Re_lo, so the bores they refuse are every bore above a threshold, and neither depends on C; so goal seek's bound builds (decision 21) still prove every trial. An entered C used as entered meets only the first. *(GOALS §4.6; D9's refusal where no cited correlation gives a physical value.)*

### Results and traceability

`SolvedState.orificeResults`, always present (`{}` without plates or when the solve did not converge): flow, headLoss, pressureLoss = ρg·headLoss (ISO 5167-2's permanent pressure loss), pressureDrop (static, as a valve's: ρgh + ρg(z_out − z_in) + ρ(v_out² − v_in²)/2, D7), differentialPressure, venaContractaPressure, beta, pipeDiameter, boreDiameter, reynoldsNumber, dischargeCoefficient (C_L), coefficientSource, coefficientReynoldsNumber, tapCoefficientRatio, lossCoefficient, taps and meterDischargeCoefficient. A closed-off plate has no result; one in a floating section has its flow, differences, reading, K and C, and no p_vc. Its trace gives the inputs with their sources (ISO's C and no taps labelled default; an entered C with its definition), eq. (4) term by term at D and D/2 tappings and at the plate's taps, the hold, the move, K, h by the law and as solved, the reading, p_vc absolute and gauge, every term of the static-drop identity, and, marked not used, the K a meter's own C would give and the C and K at L2′ = 0.125; its notes state the departures, ε = 1, the ISO and installation assumptions, the recovered outlet pressure and each flag's inequality. An "Orifices" methods section after Equipment (its entered-C items only with an entered C) and one assumption appear only when the project has a plate. *(GOALS §4.2.)*

### Scenarios and goal seek

D15's allow-list gains `boreDiameter` and `dischargeCoefficient` (null sets ISO's C, written "ISO 5167-2's (the default)"); the taps are installation, the same in every case, and not listed. Goal seek varies the bore (log) and the C (linear, the C at the plate's taps, as entered; a case on ISO's C starts in the middle of the bounds), and builds the network at each bound of such a variable (D16); four goals, `orifice_flow`, `orifice_pressure_loss`, `orifice_differential_pressure` (published with `needs: ['taps']`: 400 `GOAL_NOT_APPLICABLE` on a plate without taps) and `orifice_pressure_drop`, which are also Compare's rows. D stays fixed in a goal seek (a pipe's `spec` is not a variable).

### Design checks

None of D19's checks applies to the plate: NPSH margin and the operating region are a pump's, and pipe velocity covers the pipes on both sides of it, not its bore (`test_orifice.py::test_no_design_check_applies_to_a_plate`).

### Designers' web defaults (reversible, D14)

One menu entry, "Orifice plate (ISO 5167-2)", in the group "Orifice plates: restriction orifices, flow meters", id prefix `O`; every path that creates one asks for the bore first. The form: the bore in the diameter unit with a read-only β line (D from the inlet pipe as the form resolves it; amber at β ≥ 1), ISO's C or an entered C with its definition in the hint, and optional taps (locked in a scenario). An Orifices results view after Equipment, a Build strip of flow, permanent loss and reading, Compare rows (relative for the flow, the loss and the reading, not the static drop), goal-seek groups with default bounds [0.1 D, 0.75 D] for the bore and [v/2, min(2v, 1)] for the C (D from the inlet pipe as the case is now; with corner or flange taps every C of the box, moved, at most 0.99: the C at most 0.99/r_max, a bore box on an entered C bounded the same way, r_max decision 17 (c)'s bound from D, d and the taps, transcribed from the API in `utils/orifice-bound.ts` to pre-fill defaults only and checked against it by `test_web_orifice_r_max.py`), and an "Orifice plates" export sheet after Equipment with the bore and pipe bore each solve used, with About lines only in a file that has it.

### Versions

`METHOD_VERSION` 1.4.0: an orifice result names a version that has the plate (GOALS §4.3, as D18). Its changelog line says that projects without orifices are unchanged in every value; every link last solved with 1.3.0 shows the method-change notice after its next solve.

`METHOD_VERSION` 1.6.1 (2026-11-03; the meter refusal from its review, 2026-11-17): rule (d)'s slope test and rule (e) hold wherever the plate is, so a plate the solve never evaluates (in a section that closed valves close off) past the slope bound or with 4ρ/(π D μ) not finite, and one whose meter coefficient fluids cannot evaluate at its taps' own Re_lo where the results never give its reading (closed off, or a system curve through it), which solved on 1.6.0, are now 422s; rule (e)'s first, second and fourth cases were internal errors (500) wherever the plate was, and its third and fifth where the solve or the results evaluated it (Limits above; D9, TSD §12 item 19). A solve that stops on a value that is not a finite number names the plate and its K/(2gA²) at that flow (D7). The corpus comparison found no other difference (D7).

`METHOD_VERSION` 1.6.2 (2026-11-26; proposal `open-defects-2.md` §3 A1): rule (d)'s first test is retaken at the least C the law takes (Limits above). The changelog: "An orifice plate's coefficient test (ORIFICE_OUT_OF_RANGE: K/(2gA²) a finite positive number at every flow) is taken again, where its closed-form bound refuses the plate, at the least discharge coefficient the plate's law takes at any flow: the entered value where it is used as entered; for a value entered at corner or flange taps, that value times the least ratio of the two tap sets' ISO 5167-2 coefficients over every Reynolds number from the lowest; for ISO 5167-2's own coefficient, its value at D and D/2 tappings at infinite Reynolds number. A coefficient entered at corner or flange taps in a band just above where the refusal began (coefficients of about 10⁻¹⁵⁵ to 10⁻¹⁴⁷ on pipes of 1 mm to 1 m) was refused and is now accepted, or refused by the slope test instead; no plate is newly refused. A plate still refused quotes the least coefficient its law takes." Measured on the build, by bisection over the doubles of C_e on 449 moved plates (corner and flange taps, D 10⁻⁶⁰ m to 10 m, β 0.05 to rule (b)'s limit):
- the changed band is from about 3·10⁻¹² to 78 % of the edge wide, widest at β 0.97 to rule (b)'s limit, where the closed-form bound took the ratio as 1 and the least ratio is at least 1.75; every plate measured has one;
- on pipes of 1 mm to 1 m, with β from 0.05, the new edge in C_e runs from about 1.5·10⁻¹⁵⁵ (1 m) to 8.7·10⁻¹⁴⁷ (1 mm);
- the band is refused by the slope test instead, a different 422, where K at the largest C_L can pass half the largest double while K/(2gA²) at the least is finite: K ∝ 1/C² for such a C, so where min(1, 2gA²)·(least r/r_max)² exceeds 1/2, from D about 0.45 m (review of 2026-11-26: at β 0.8 every sampled band C_e on D 0.46 m was refused so, none on 0.45 m; on D 1, 3 and 10 m every plate measured up to β 0.9), not where 2gA² passes 1 (D 0.536 m);
