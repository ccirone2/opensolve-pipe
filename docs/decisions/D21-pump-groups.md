### Owner's answers

A group's curves are defined between its station's outer ports S and D: the group curve (slice 4a) and the group's own system curve (4b). The catalogue composite curve (flows added at equal flange head) is not drawn: on the design's probes it crosses the system 6–10 % beyond the duty. Groups are page state, never stored (`schemaVersion` stays 1). No impeller-trim or ISO 9906 inputs in slice 4: a trimmed impeller is the maker's curve, entered as its own library curve. *(GOALS §3, §4.1.)*

### Station

The smallest part of the network holding every member that meets the rest at exactly two places, S and D (ports, or a reservoir or reference node taken whole, never a tee or wye centre), with no fixed head, assigned flow or other pump inside: a minimum vertex cut between the members and those barriers (Menger's theorem), searched again toward a single place that is not a pump's own port (the outside closing a loop), that place itself a candidate too (a reservoir or reference node taken whole, for pumps that return to the tank they draw from, only where every pump faces one way between the places: other pairs with it circulate a pump through the tank), with some member on a simple path from S to D. Arrangement parallel, series or mixed from the parts the station falls into without its members; S is the side they face away from (where none tells the sides apart, the side the duty's flow enters; at a duty without flow, within the solver's continuity tolerance, the finder's order). Otherwise 422 `NOT_A_STATION`, every reason an issue in words with its ids: two places not found (`NO_SEPARATING_PORTS`), or no member on a way between them (`NO_WAY_THROUGH`); an assigned flow, another pump or a fixed head inside; members on different boundaries; fewer members facing D → S than S → D (`MEMBER_REVERSED`) or as many each way (`MEMBERS_OPPOSED`); an active PRV or PSV inside, listed also beside an assigned flow, other pump or fixed head inside and beside reversed members. The station's extent is topology only, the same in every case; its PRVs and PSVs are read with each case's statuses, so a scenario can refuse a station the base accepts. *(GOALS §4.2.)*

### Duty and group curve

The duty is the case's own solve, bit for bit `/solve`'s: Q_st from S into the station, ΔH_st = EGL(D) − EGL(S), shares (none in parallel at a duty without flow), each parallel member's loss on its way H_i − ΔH_st and the series interstage term ΣH_i − ΔH_st, the head across the elements between the pumps: their losses, or the head a closed valve holds (exact: each is a sum of EGL differences). The group curve is the station solved alone (`BuiltNetwork.held_closed` holds every outside link closed; empty for every network `build_network` makes), S at the duty's EGL(S) and D a rise above, every element on its own law and rules (a relief valve inside too, started closed: its set is a pressure difference, D23), from the duty's rise outward; the duty check (0.5 % or 10⁻⁶ m³/s) guards D13. No duty when S or D is closed off, or when their levels cannot be compared (floating sections). `POST /api/v1/pump-group-curve`, with a "Pump group" methods section; it leaves `METHOD_VERSION` as it is (1.4.0 then; 1.5.0 since D22, 1.6.0 since D23): no solved value changes. *(GOALS §3: exact at every rise, no assumed split.)*

### Group system curve (slice 4b)

`curve: 'system'`: H_sys(Q) = EGL(D) − EGL(S), the rise the rest of the network needs when the station's flow Q is forced from S to D: everything inside held closed and one virtual fixed-flow link from S to D (`system_network`), every element outside on its own law and rules as at a pump's system-curve point (an active relief valve starting open, D23). It is D10's system curve with the station in place of one pump, by the same code (`system_curve.py` takes a forced element with its words and its pumps), so a station of one pump gives its pump's `/system-curve` bit for bit at every flow of the grid but the duty's own (where Q_st is a grid flow, its point is solved warm from the case's solve: within D6 of `/system-curve`'s, or another state under D13), and apart from that point it depends on nothing inside (the members' statuses, speeds, controls and shares; an active PRV or PSV inside does not refuse it). Flows from min(0, Q_st) to 1.2 × the largest of Q_st and the group curve's flows (Q_st alone with an active PRV or PSV inside; where none of those is above 10⁻⁶ m³/s, 1.2 × the sum over the members of their curves' largest flows × their speeds: no net flow through the station, whether every member is off or members run without carrying flow from S to D), plus Q_st, solved first warm from the case's solve; then the grid as `/system-curve` solves it, with D10's nine reasons, retries and data in the station's words, and no NPSHa. The system duty check (D6's 0.5 % or 0.01 m of ΔH_st) guards D13: it says why a point has no rise, and, where the duty's first start alone leaves the rise undetermined (the station alone sets a level there) and D10's retries find another state, says so. It leaves `METHOD_VERSION` as it is (1.6.0): `/solve` and `/system-curve` are unchanged. *(GOALS §4.2: one definition of H_sys.)*
